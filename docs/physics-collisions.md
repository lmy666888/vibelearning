# Momentum & Collisions — independent event model

Version 1 registers `momentum-collisions` with the shared workspace and AI contracts. Scientific computation is deterministic and provider-independent. It does not use the electromagnetic stepper. Modes are exact 1D two-body collisions and frictionless, nonrotating 2D disks. No gravity, walls, friction, spin, deformation, multi-body or simultaneous contacts are modeled. Initial overlap is rejected, not silently corrected. In 1D, all transverse positions and velocities must be zero; use a preset to change modes without an invalid intermediate configuration.

## Parameters and equations

SI bounds: masses .1–10 kg, radii .1–2 m, each initial position ±20 m, each velocity component ±20 m/s, duration .1–20 s, restitution 0–1, dimension choice 1/2, frame choice 0 laboratory /1 center of mass. Numeric discrete choices must be integral. All query times are finite in [0,duration]; query/body schemas reject extra fields, booleans and invalid types. No output rounding is used in the engine.

P=mA vA+mB vB; K=(mA|vA|²+mB|vB|²)/2. For an isolated pair, momentum is conserved. Define r=xB−xA, u=vB−vA, R=rA+rB. Contact solves |r+ut|²=R². Separating/static pairs and tangency with zero impulse produce no event. In 1D, t=(|r_x|−R)/|u_x|. In 2D use discriminant/4=|u|²R²−(r×u)² and stable smaller root t=(|r|²−R²)/(−r·u+sqrt(discriminant/4)); this avoids the quadratic subtraction/cancellation for a small positive contact time. Exactly touching approaching pairs collide at t=0. Only contacts within the observation window are reported.

Contact normal n=(xB−xA)/|xB−xA|, normal relative speed u_n=u·n<0, impulse J=−(1+e)u_n/(1/mA+1/mB). 2D: vA′=vA−Jn/mA; vB′=vB+Jn/mB. Tangential velocities are unchanged. 1D explicitly uses analytical vA′=((mA−e mB)vA+(1+e)mB vB)/(mA+mB), vB′=((mB−e mA)vB+(1+e)mA vA)/(mA+mB). Equal-mass velocity swapping is a consequence only for elastic 1D, not a general rule.

Expected collision energy loss: (mA mB/(mA+mB))(1−e²)u_n²/2. e=1 conserves kinetic energy; e<1 dissipates normal relative kinetic energy. In 1D e=0 produces co-moving bodies at fixed contact separation. **2D e=0 is not a permanent sticking constraint**: tangential relative motion persists, and disks can separate. This distinction appears in the UI and education metadata.

Positions are piecewise straight lines before/after the single exact event. An isolated separating pair needs at most one impulse; there is no per-frame overlap correction, tunneling, repeated impulse or resolution loop. Time sampling and replay partition cannot change the scientific event. No collision in the window is explicit (`collision_time:null`), not a fabricated time.

## Frames, observables and visualization

Vcm=P/(mA+mB), Rcm(0)=(mA xA+mB xB)/(mA+mB). Display coordinates in COM mode subtract Rcm(0)+Vcm t, display velocities subtract Vcm. Physical parameters/state and clearly labeled laboratory momentum/energy remain laboratory quantities. Display momentum/energy are separately labeled. Collision marker is transformed at the event time. Frame change is not a physical impulse. COM kinetic energy is K_lab−|P|²/[2(mA+mB)].

Renderer draws two labeled disks (physical radii, minimum2px visibility), blue velocity and amber momentum arrows with explicit separate SI display scales, contact point, start/end ghosts and a connector for co-moving 1D e=0. Timeline, exact-contact seek, 0.1s step, play/pause/reset, mass/velocity/restitution controls, collision type selector and laboratory/COM choice share existing conventions. Signed before/after momentum-component and energy charts include text; table gives laboratory velocities. No fabricated telemetry is plotted. Tiny negative floating energy losses ~1e−16 J may remain visible for elastic collisions; tests use explicit error bounds rather than clamping evidence away.

Seven presets: equal-mass elastic, unequal-mass elastic, stationary target, partially inelastic, perfectly inelastic 1D, oblique2D and COM frame. All use one selector, AI policy/gateway, typed parameter/preset actions, deterministic previews, explicit confirmation, stale invalidation, lesson generation/observation and versioned paused recovery. No event histories or credentials are persisted or sent as unbounded AI context. Preview is evaluated at initial query but includes predicted contact time/post velocities/post energy for the selected observation horizon. Supported AI examples: massA×2, restitution=.5 or1, elastic-vs-inelastic lesson.
